<objective>
Build the two enrichment jobs in `internal/backendsrv/enrich/jobs/` — the daily PigParse price job and the weekly P1999 wiki job — by composing the Plan 03 polite client + the Plan 02 parsers + the Plan 01 store methods. These replace the Apps Script `refresh*.ts` triggers' I/O wrappers; the Sheets-specific machinery (6-minute resumable cursor, `monitorCellCount`, `weeklySchemaHealthcheck`, `LockService`, `PropertiesService`, post-run view rebuilds) is DELETED, not ported. Also add the one read-side store helper the wiki items pass needs: the distinct `(item_id, name)` union from `inventory_item`.

Purpose: This is where fetch → parse → upsert composes over one transaction, mirroring `ingest/handler.go::bindAndReplace`'s single-tested-SQL-path discipline. The jobs own the two genuine Go-vs-Sheet behaviors: the truncation guard becomes a LOG (not an abort — D-4 graceful degradation), and the PigParse t=0/t=1 dedup (keep WTS) lives HERE as one isolated filter (D-9 reversibility), not in the parser. This plan depends on all three Wave 1 plans (schema/store, parsers, politefetch) — it is Wave 2.
Output: `jobs/pigparse.go`, `jobs/wiki.go`, `jobs/urls.go` (the hardcoded API URL constants), `store/itemids.go`, and their tests (httptest-served fixtures + NewTestDB).
