# SquireBot

A small app that streams your EverQuest (Project 1999) inventory and spellbook to your guild's shared website at [squirebot.quest](https://squirebot.quest). Runs on **Windows** (system-tray app) and **Linux** (headless daemon, for P99 under WINE).

**Download the latest release:** [Windows installer](https://github.com/boejowen/SquireBot/releases/latest/download/SquireBot-Setup.exe) · [Linux tarball](https://github.com/boejowen/SquireBot/releases/latest/download/squirebot-linux-amd64.tar.gz)
